夢叶神社 BGM「こもれびの願い」

Original composition, arrangement and stereo production for Yumekana Shrine.
96 seconds. D major pentatonic (D E F# A B), soft piano and flute,
a quiet original plucked-string texture and synthesized air.
No third-party song or field recording is included.

Piano and flute instrument recordings:
University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples (MIS)
Created by Lawrence Fritts, Director of the Electronic Music Studios,
Professor of Composition, University of Iowa.

Official source and permission:
https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISpiano.html
https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISflute.html

Permission stated on the official MIS page (checked 2026-11-04):
"Since 1997, these recordings have been freely available on this website
and may be downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions."

Samples used: Piano.pp D3 A3 B3 D4 E4 Gb4 A4 B4 D5 E5,
and flute.nonvib.pp B3B4 / B4Bb5 chromatic scales.
The instrument recordings remain credited to their original creator.
The arrangement uses newly composed phrases; the downloaded recordings
are individual instrument samples, not a pre-existing musical piece.

Production source: scripts/render-shrine-bgm.py
Source hashes and output metadata: komorebi-no-negai.json
Encoding: MP3 128 kbps, stereo 44.1 kHz; nominal mastering target -22 LUFS.
No paid music service, subscription, API key or external playback service
is required. The app plays its own same-origin file only after a user action.
